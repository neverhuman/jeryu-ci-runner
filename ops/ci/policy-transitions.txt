# Authorized audit-policy transitions.
#
# The proof lane scores every head with protected main's own
# agent/audit-policy.toml, so a head may not bring its own scoring policy. A
# head whose policy differs from protected main's is refused unless this file
# -- read from the protected-main clone, never from the head under test --
# pins that exact change as one line:
#
#   <protected sha256> <candidate sha256>
#
# both of `sha256sum agent/audit-policy.toml`, protected main's first. A
# transition therefore lands in two steps: one change adds the pin here and
# merges, then the change carrying the new policy bytes merges against it. A
# pin may not lower minimum_score; raising the merge floor needs no pin on the
# protected side, only the pinned bytes.
#
# Blank lines and #-comments are ignored. Any other line shape refuses the
# whole file: a pin nobody can parse must not become a pin nobody checks.
#
# No transition is authorized right now.
